# Introduction to Suspension Design

A beginner-friendly guide to understanding suspension geometry and the thought process behind designing a double wishbone suspension.

### Topics Covered
- Camber, Toe, Castor & Scrub Radius
- Suspension geometry and its effects
- Double wishbone kinematics
- Graphical analysis and hardpoint selection

### Scope
This is an introductory guide focused on **geometry and kinematics**. Detailed force analysis, structural design, spring-damper selection, suspension dynamics, tyre mechanics, and multibody dynamics are not covered.

**All the best with your suspension design! 🚗**
